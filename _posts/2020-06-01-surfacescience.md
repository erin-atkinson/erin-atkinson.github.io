Worked with the Cavendish Laboratory Surfaces group on an online summer project. My role was to investigate reconstruction methods for under-sampled scanning helium microscope images. I learned about the physics and engineering of the Cavendish SHeM and introduced myself to machine learning, implementing a model for a Bayesian dictionary learning reconstruction method in MATLAB.
