I graduated from my BA and MSci degrees at Fitzwilliam College. 

My Master's project was undertaken with the British Antarctic Survey. The aim was to continue previous work, automatically classifying Southern Ocean regions based on temperature data. Within the project, I demonstrated a method for utilising simulated climate data to determine the appropriate number of regions, which was previously done in an ambiguous manner. This component of my thesis was worked into a paper by my supervisor, Dr Emma Boland (to whom I'm very grateful for both putting up with me and polishing the write-up while I was busy moving to Toronto...)
